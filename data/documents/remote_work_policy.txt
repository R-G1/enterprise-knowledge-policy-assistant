REMOTE WORK POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which eligible employees may work remotely and the responsibilities associated with remote work.

2. Eligibility

Employees whose roles can be performed effectively outside the office may be eligible for remote work. Eligibility is determined by the employee's role, team requirements, performance, and manager approval.

3. Remote Work Allowance

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Specific schedules should be agreed with the employee's manager and may be adjusted according to business requirements.

4. Approval

Employees must obtain manager approval before beginning a regular remote-work arrangement. A remote-work request should specify the proposed working days and any relevant scheduling considerations.

5. Working Hours

Employees working remotely are expected to follow their normal working hours and remain reachable through approved company communication tools during those hours.

6. Work Environment

Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable and secure workspace. Company information must not be exposed to unauthorized individuals when working outside the office.

7. Security

Employees must use approved company devices and follow all applicable security requirements while working remotely. Sensitive company information should not be stored on personal devices unless explicitly authorized.

8. Office Attendance

Managers may require employees to attend the office for meetings, collaboration sessions, training, or other business requirements. Employees should follow reasonable office-attendance requests from their manager.

9. Changes to Remote Work Arrangements

Managers may temporarily change or suspend remote-work arrangements when business, operational, security, or collaboration requirements make office attendance necessary.

10. Contact

Questions about remote-work eligibility or scheduling should be directed to the employee's manager or HR.
