ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy explains the annual leave entitlement and leave request process for employees.

2. Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave is intended to provide employees with time away from work for rest, personal activities, and planned commitments.

3. Leave Requests

Employees should submit planned annual leave requests at least 5 working days before the requested start date. Leave is subject to manager approval and operational requirements.

4. Emergency Leave

Emergency leave may be requested without the standard 5-working-day notice period when an unexpected personal or family circumstance occurs. Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

5. Leave Approval

Submitting a leave request does not automatically mean that leave has been approved. Employees should wait for confirmation from their manager before making commitments that depend on the leave being approved.

6. Carry Forward

Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days should normally be used by the end of the first quarter of the following year.

7. Leave Cancellation

An approved leave request may be cancelled or modified when business requirements change. Employees should discuss changes with their manager as soon as possible.

8. Contact

Questions about leave balances or policy interpretation should be directed to the HR team.
