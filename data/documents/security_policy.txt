INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

1. Purpose

This policy defines basic security practices employees must follow when accessing and handling company information and systems.

2. Password Requirements

Employees must use strong, unique passwords for company accounts. Passwords must not be shared with other employees or stored in plain text. Employees should use the company's approved password-management solution when available.

3. Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for systems and services that require it. Employees must not approve an MFA request that they did not initiate.

4. Confidential Information

Confidential company information must only be accessed by authorized employees for legitimate business purposes. Employees must not send confidential information to personal email accounts or unauthorized external recipients.

5. Devices

Company devices must be protected with the required security controls, including screen locking and approved endpoint protection. Employees must report lost or stolen company devices promptly.

6. Phishing and Suspicious Messages

Employees should not open suspicious attachments, click unknown links, or provide credentials in response to unexpected requests. Suspicious messages should be reported through the company's approved security-reporting process.

7. Security Incidents

Any suspected security incident, unauthorized access, data exposure, or lost company device must be reported to the security or IT team as soon as possible.

8. Remote Access

Employees accessing company systems remotely must use approved access mechanisms and follow the organization's remote-work security requirements.

9. Access Control

Employees should only access systems, applications, and information necessary for their assigned responsibilities. Access must not be shared with another person.

10. Contact

Security questions and suspected incidents should be reported to the IT or Information Security team.
